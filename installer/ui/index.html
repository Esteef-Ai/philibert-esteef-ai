    Install Philibert

    Philibert is an AI assistant that lives on a drive - or in a folder on this computer. The AI models, your notes and your settings all stay there, and nothing is installed on the computer itself. Plug the drive into a Windows PC or a Mac and it is the same Philibert.

    Takes
    About 5 minutes of questions, then downloading - from a few minutes to an hour, depending on the models you pick and your connection.
    Needs
    The Internet while installing. Afterwards Philibert runs offline.
    Space
    From about 12 GB (one small model) to 80 GB (every model).

    This computer

    Setup looked at what this computer has, so it can tell you which AI models will run well here.

    Which computers will you use it on?

    Philibert's AI models, notes and settings are shared. Only the part that runs it is different for Windows and Mac - pick both and the drive works anywhere.
