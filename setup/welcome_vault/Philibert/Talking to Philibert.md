# Talking to Philibert

Type in the box at the bottom left and press **Enter**. Philibert answers in the
thread above it, and the board in the middle shows what it is doing while it works.

- **Talk instead of typing:** the microphone button records one message; the round
  button starts conversation mode, where you just speak.
- **Pick a model:** the list at the top of the chat. Bigger models answer better
  and need a stronger graphics card. The coloured dots in the installer told you
  which ones suit this computer.
- **Unload GPU:** frees the graphics card for games or other apps; Philibert loads
  the model again on your next message.

Ask it to make a note and it writes one here, in the vault - see [[How notes work]].
