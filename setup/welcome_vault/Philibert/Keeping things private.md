# Keeping things private

- **Offline by default.** Philibert answers with the models on this drive. Nothing
  you type is sent anywhere.
- **The Internet switch** at the top of the chat lets Philibert search the web or
  open pages for you. It starts OFF every time; turn it on only when you want it.
- **Your notes stay here,** in `data\vault` on the drive. Nothing is copied onto the
  computer you plug into, and Philibert's window keeps its own settings on the drive.
- **Unplugging** is safe once Philibert's window is closed.
