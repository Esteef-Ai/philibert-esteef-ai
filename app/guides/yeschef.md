# Yes Chef! guide (for Philibert)

Philibert reads this when someone asks for help inside Yes Chef!. Write what is true; keep answers short.

## What the app does
- Recipes are kept by weight (grams). Baker's percentages: every ingredient as a % of the total flour.
- Auto-scale: typing any amount (or the total flour, a multiplier, or loaves x dough weight) rescales the
  whole recipe. With auto-scale off, a typed amount changes the recipe itself.
- Units: grams, ounces, or cups and spoons (cups/spoons are approximate; weighing is better).
- Forks: a copy of a recipe with one change, to bake and compare. Troubleshoot makes a fork with one
  targeted change for a problem (dense, chewy, gummy, pale crust, flat, collapsed, burst, bland).
- Batches: several at once, each with its own colour, steps and timers. Wait and oven steps start their
  timer by themselves; hands-on steps have a Start button. Cook mode keeps the screen on.
- Import: a link (needs INTERNET on), a PDF, a Word .docx, a screenshot or photo, or pasted text.
  Amounts are converted to grams; check them, above all after a photo (text recognition can misread 7 as 1).
- MUST READ (red): overnight or long waits, fridge time, make-ahead steps, pre-ferments/starters, very long
  total time, "Important" notes. A batch can't start until they are ticked as read.
- MUST CHECK (amber): early preheats, steps that go by look not time, divided ingredients, water
  temperature, room-temperature ingredients, equipment, explicit "do not" warnings.

## Baking answers that are usually right
- Dense crumb: under-proofed (most common), under-developed gluten (mix to windowpane), too little water,
  or shaped too tightly. Change one thing per bake.
- Too chewy: high-protein flour; swap some bread flour for all-purpose, or add a little fat (2-4%).
- Gummy: under-baked (bake to 205-210°F inside for lean bread), sliced warm, or too much diastatic malt.
- Pale crust: not enough sugar/malt, oven not hot enough, or no steam early then no dry heat late.
- Over-proofed: collapses when scored, spreads flat; the poke springs back not at all. Shorten the proof.
- Under-proofed: poke springs back fast; bursts on the side in the oven.
- Water for instant yeast: lukewarm (about 95-105°F) is fine; above about 130°F kills yeast.

## When something in the app goes wrong
- "INTERNET is off" when importing a link: turn INTERNET on (top right in Philibert), or save the page
  as a PDF and add that.
- "The site answered 403": the site blocks automatic downloads. Paste the text, or save as PDF.
- A picture reads badly: take it straight on, in good light, one page at a time; or paste the text.
- "No chat model" / "No picture-reading model": add one in Models (the picture model is called Eyes).
